# What's new on Infinite Island 🌴

Your version number is shown under the title when the game starts.

## 0.42.0: The overgrown castle maze

- Wildroot Hollow now has winding stone passages, looping cloisters, a fern library, ivy courtyard and throne chamber, plus three dead ends with a pantry, well and flower shrine.
- Pale flagstones, ruined crenellated walls, broken arches and crumbling pillars replace the garden platforms. Moss, trailing vines, ferns, flowers and mushrooms reclaim the castle.
- Walls block movement, attacks and enemy sight. Alerted guardians navigate around hallway corners; enemies are spread through the maze and its side chambers.
- The portal code, party travel, combat styles, HP resets, food healing and clearing reward still work. All 296 Node tests pass; browser checks reach every room and verify wall blocking.

## 0.41.0: Wildroot Hollow

- Strike the numbered standing stones **1, 1, 1, 3** to open a portal. Enter together to bring the whole party into a cozy alternate garden, including friends on other screens.
- Fight nine determined garden guardians: berries, carrots, turnips, sprouts and a Pumpkin Patchkeeper. Golden rings warn where attacks will land.
- Choose swords with three-hit combos, sweeping axes or slow stunning hammers. Four sets of weapons wait in the safe entrance.
- Each player has 100 HP; any defeat resets the party and dungeon. Eat cooked dishes outside combat to heal 25/45/65 HP depending on their island. Return home together through the entrance portal.
- Clear the dungeon for three crystals and five herbs per run. Inventory survives resets; dungeon progress is session-only.
- Verified with 290 Node tests and browser checks for stone impacts, entry, tools, attacks, healing, party resets, return travel and a joining second screen.

## 0.40.0: Flower garlands

- Garden-hearth feasts award 24 flower garlands: natural, pearl / gold, and rainbow versions for all eight flowers. Bloom pairs and garden upgrades unlock the higher levels; rainbow garlands glow at night.
- A bamboo stand appears with your first garland. Try them on alongside hats and tails; your choice is remembered and visible to other screens.
- All qualifying garlands unlock together after the hat reveal. Shared unlocks save alongside the garden, with old saves and pose packets supported.

## 0.39.0: The garden grows

- Bring materials to Mossback after planting your first seed. Three shared jobs build bamboo trellises and stepping stones, waterfall irrigation, and a woven lantern pavilion.
- Jobs open eight beds, then all sixteen. Irrigation makes tended seeds and planted cuttings bud immediately, ready to bloom at the next dawn.
- Breadfruit trees now drop breadfruit logs. Use them for garden jobs or fuel at any campfire. Partial donations and completed stages save with the garden.

## 0.38.2: A quest for every campfire

- The Bag hides locked islands and empty supplies, and lists your current island immediately after Across islands.
- Keep a home, Frostpine and Emberleaf feast quest active together. Each campfire offers its own regional feasts, and you return to that fire to prepare them.
- The tracker and quest-needed bag chips follow your camera's region. Give up cancels only the quest at the campfire you are visiting; the redundant quest-list menu row is removed.
- Feasts still gather everyone, share hat progress and unlock Frostpine tails at the camp. Old quest saves migrate without losing progress.

## 0.38.0: Plant, tend and watch them bloom

- **Plant your seeds!** Walk up to an open garden bed and choose a seed you have found. Each seed can be planted once. It needs a little care first: **tend** it once with the right gift and it sprouts. Two flowers share each gift:
  - **Ash** (Ember Lily, Sunbell): break the dark **basalt rocks** and they crumble to 1-2 ash on top of their stone.
  - **Coconut of spring water** (Lagoon Lotus, Steam Fern): stand in the **hot spring** and choose "Fill a coconut" (it uses a coconut from your bag).
  - **Mulch** (Glow Hibiscus, Starfruit Vine): fell a **banana tree** and it leaves 1-2 mulch next to its logs.
  - **Taro** (Moon Orchid, Firefly Plumeria): dig it up with the shovel, as before.
  Try to tend without the gift and Mossback tells you what is needed and where to get it.
- **The garden grows with the night.** A tended seed is a sprout. When **dusk** comes it closes into a **bud**, and at **dawn**, after the whole night, it **opens into a bloom** with a little sparkle. It only grows while the day really turns: not while the sun is held still, not while the island sleeps, and not while the page is closed (no catching up). Reload in the middle of the night and nothing is counted twice.
- **Eight different blooms**, each in its own colours and shape: lilies and lotuses with pointed petals, round orchids and hibiscus with a stamen, nodding sunbells, a fern with glowing spore tips, a star-shaped starfruit. They **open a little by day and glow softly at night**, and **fireflies gather over the garden** after dark.
- **Take a cutting.** A flower in bloom gives you **one cutting**. Plant it in another bed and you get a second one of that flower, already rooted (no gift needed). The first plant keeps blooming.
- **Ask Mossback** "Plant a seed": he now tells you what you can plant and what each plant needs next. "How do they grow?" has the whole story.
- Everything is shared and saved by the host, so everyone on the island sees the same garden (even if they join in the middle of the night), and two people planting the same bed at once is settled fairly: one planting wins.

## 0.37.1: Eight seeds to find

- **The Night-Bloom seeds are hiding all over Emberleaf!** Eight rare flowers once grew on the fire mountain and their seeds are scattered: **Ember Lily, Moon Orchid, Lagoon Lotus, Steam Fern, Glow Hibiscus, Starfruit Vine, Sunbell and Firefly Plumeria**. Each one is found in a different way and place. Some wait at a little glint on the ground (bring a **shovel** for the digging ones), one is tucked in the steam of the hot spring, one is in a quiet spot behind the waterfall, and a few only show themselves **at dusk or at night**: a tree that glows after dark, a hibiscus bud that lights up, fireflies gathering over one special plumeria.
- **Every find plays a sparkle and a jingle** and shows a toast on every player's screen ("Found Moon Orchid seeds! (3 / 8)"). Each seed can only be found once, together, and found seeds are saved with the island. Try the wrong time of day and you only get a gentle hint.
- **Ask Mossback** "Where are the seeds?": he lists what you have found and whispers a hint for each seed still missing (including when to look for the night and dusk ones). "Plant a seed" already names the seeds you have; the planting itself comes next.

## 0.37.0: Mossback and the garden plots

- **Meet Mossback!** An old, kind **tortoise gardener** with a mossy shell (and a little flower growing on it), a straw sun hat and a small watering can lives at the Emberleaf terrace garden. He turns up once the Emberleaf bridge is built. When you first see him he is **dozing** by the empty beds (with a floating "z") and wakes up when you walk close. He turns slowly to face you, blinks slowly, tips his watering can now and then, and gets sleepy at night.
- **Talk to him** to hear how Emberleaf once glowed at night with rare flowers, until a storm scattered the seeds. Ask him how the flowers grow, or about the seeds and the garden jobs (he has no news about those yet).
- **Sixteen garden plots** line the three terraces: mounded dark soil in little bamboo frames. Only **four are open** for now; the other twelve are overgrown with weeds and fenced off with a crossed-bamboo marker. Look at an open bed and Mossback tells you that it needs a seed first. (Planting comes next!)
- Under the hood, the plots are shared and saved by the host, so everybody on the island sees the same garden, even people who join late. Nothing grows yet.

## 0.36.1: Emberleaf's waterfall

- **A waterfall on the fire mountain!** A little spring pool near the top of the volcano's south-west slope spills down a short channel over a rock lip, falls into a **plunge pool** with a misty splash and a faint rainbow by day, and then trickles down the three terraces and across the jungle floor to the lagoon. You can see its white streak from the home island.
- **Walk behind the falling water.** A narrow rocky **ledge** hides between the curtain of water and the cliff. Reach it from the pool's west bank and you stay visible through the water. (Something may grow there one day.)
- The deep middle of the pool is too deep to wade; its shore, the cascade and the stream are shallow and walkable, and the garden trail crosses the stream at a shallow ford.
- A soft rushing sound gets louder as you walk up to the pool.
- Home's stream looks exactly as before.

## 0.36.0: Tails, and Frostpine's story is complete

- **Twelve tails to wear!** Fox, squirrel, rabbit and grouse, each in three levels: the basic animal, a recoloured one and a **rainbow** one. They hang on your lower back, swish when you stand still and wag when you run, and they are bigger than before so you can spot them from the normal camera.
- **How to get them:** hold a feast at the **Frostpine camp fire**. When it ends, every tail you have earned unlocks at once. One critter of a species living at the camp gives that animal's basic tail; **both** critters of a species give the second tail once the camp has comfort 1, and the rainbow one at comfort 3. Skipped levels unlock too, so a cosy camp full of friends can bring up to all twelve in one feast. Feasts at the home fire or the Emberleaf hearth do not unlock tails.
- The feast banner announces it ("New tail: Fox Tail!" or "4 new tails!" with their icons popping in one by one) and a toast does the same, on every screen.
- **The tail stump** appears at the camp with your first tail: a carved hollow stump with the unlocked tails peeking out. Press Interact on it to try the next tail (and back to none), with a hop, a poof and a name tag. It works for every player.
- Your choice is remembered on your screen, other screens see your tail on your character, and players who join later see the stump and everyone's tails. Starting a new game resets the unlocks.

## 0.31.8: The camp gets built

- Finishing a **camp job** now really builds something. A sparkle at the fire, then the new pieces **drop in one after another** with a squash, a puff of snow dust and a rising thunk, and the critters at the camp hop for joy. Everyone in the game sees it happen.
- **Comfort 1, windbreak and log benches:** a curved wall of stacked logs behind the fire, a woodpile, and **proper log benches instead of the cold stumps**. The feast sits everyone on the new benches, still facing the camera.
- **Comfort 2, cozy dens and lantern posts:** a straw-and-pine-bough den for every critter, tall **lantern posts with glowing crystals** (they shine at night), a feeding trough and strings of colourful bunting.
- **Comfort 3, the grove shelter:** a snowy wooden **A-frame shelter** at the side of the camp (walk under it: you still show through the roof), crystal lanterns hanging inside, boxes of **edelweiss**, and young pines planted round the clearing so the camp becomes a grove.
- Players can walk all round the camp: the fire, the benches, the dens and the trail entrance are never blocked. The camp is rebuilt as it was after a reload, and players who join later see it finished.

## 0.31.7: Camp jobs

- Chopping a Frostpine **spruce, pine or sapling** now gives **pine logs** and chopping a **birch** gives **birch logs** (new items, each with its own icon and ground model). Home and Emberleaf trees still give plain logs.
- Pine and birch logs **burn in any campfire** just like plain logs. "Add a log" counts every kind of log in your bag and uses plain logs first, then pine, then birch.
- The **Frostpine Fire Spirit** now has real **Camp jobs**. Once a critter lives at the camp, open the camp fire's menu, choose **Camp jobs**, then **Bring materials** and hand over what each job needs (Give 1, Give 5 or Give all). Three jobs in order: the windbreak and log benches (pine logs, stone, birch logs), cozy dens and lantern posts (birch logs, pinecones, lingonberries, crystals) and the grove shelter (pine logs, stone, snow truffles, crystals).
- Finishing a job raises the camp's **comfort** (shown in a toast to every player) with a sparkle at the fire and a cheer from the spirit. The camp itself doesn't change yet: the new benches, dens and shelter are coming next.
- Jobs are shared by everyone in the game (two players giving at the same time can't overspend the bag) and saved, so progress survives a reload and new players joining see it right away.

## 0.31.6: The critters move in

- You can now **feed the hungry critters**! Carry a critter's favourite food (fox: snow truffle, squirrel: pinecone, rabbit: mint, grouse: lingonberry) and the prompt changes from "Hungry..." to **Feed**. It takes one food from the bag.
- The critter **eats, does a happy spin, scampers off and poofs**, then pops up at its own spot in the **Frostpine camp**. Everyone gets a toast ("A fox moved into the Frostpine camp! (3 / 8)"). Feed all eight!
- At the camp the critters potter about near the fire and turn to watch you. **At night they curl up and sleep close to the fire.** Press interact on one to **Pet** it and get a heart.
- Feeding works on every screen in a shared game (two players racing for one critter with one food: only one gets it), and the residents are saved: they are still at the camp after a reload, and new players joining see them there straight away.

## 0.31.5: Hungry critters

- Eight **hungry animals** live in the wild on Frostpine: two foxes, two squirrels, two rabbits and two grouse, scattered over the island (forest edges, the lake shore, the plateau, the far meadows and below the peaks). Go exploring to find them!
- Each one wanders a few steps around its home and blinks, hops and looks about. They are solid, so you can't walk through them.
- Walk up to one and it **hops and thinks of its favourite food** in a thought bubble: the fox wants a snow truffle, the squirrel a pinecone, the rabbit mint and the grouse a lingonberry. Their prompt says "Hungry..." for now; feeding them comes next.

## 0.31.4: The Emberleaf terrace garden

- Under the fire mountain on Emberleaf there is a **terrace garden**: three tiers of bare, dark volcanic soil with cracked old stone edging, a few weeds and a broken bamboo trellis. Nothing is planted yet, but it looks like somebody once kept a garden here.
- A **garden hearth** sits on the middle terrace: a ring of dark basalt stones with four flat stone seats. It is a real fire of its own: feed it logs, ask it for quests and **cook Emberleaf's four dishes** there (Grilled Banana, Taro & Coconut Bake, Pineapple Skewer, Breadfruit Roast). Once the Emberleaf bridge is built, **Emberleaf Luau** and **Jungle Skewer Picnic** can be your feast quest. **Prepare the feast** at the hearth and everyone sits on the stones with the fire mountain behind them.
- The **Hearth Spirit** says hello the first time you open the hearth's menu.
- A winding **earth trail** leads from the bridge landing to the garden (a good half-minute of running), with a **bamboo torch** every so often that glows at night. Signposts at the landing and the garden point the way ("Garden" one way, "Bridge" the other).
- Emberleaf now has **five tools** lying around: an axe and a shovel not far from the landing, a hammer and a sword along the trail and a spare shovel near the garden.
- Space is kept clear for what comes next: the garden's plots, and a waterfall and stream on the mountain's south-west side. Emberleaf's trees, rocks, flowers, pineapples and dig spots were nudged a little to make room.

## 0.31.3: The Frostpine camp

- Frostpine has a **camp**: a ring of snow-dusted stones round a camp fire, four tree-stump seats and a clearing of trampled snow, on the meadow a short run north of the bridge landing.

- The camp fire is a real fire of its own: feed it logs, ask it for quests and **cook Frostpine's dishes** there (Forest Stew, Lingonberry Tart, Toasted Pine Nuts). Once the Frostpine bridge is built, **Frostpine Supper** and **Snowberry Tea Party** can be your feast quest.

- Frostpine's trees, boulders, flowers, berries, mushrooms and tools were nudged a little to make room for the camp and the trail.

## 0.25.0: Play together from your own phone

- **See yourself behind trees.** When a tree or rock hides your character, a coloured outline shows where you are.

## 0.16.0: Welcome to Infinite Island!

The first numbered version of the island. Here's what you can do:

- **Explore a cozy island:**
  - A sandy beach, meadows and a little plateau.
  - A pond and a stream.
  - Cherry blossom trees, palms, flowers, butterflies and drifting petals.
- **Play your way:**
  - Keyboard (WASD to move, E to interact, Q to cancel).
  - A gamepad, including GameCube and Wii controller adapters.
  - Your phone as a wireless controller: just scan the QR code.
- **Up to 4 friends on one screen**, each with their own colour.
- **Find tools around the island:** an axe, sword, hammer, shovel and three lightsabers. Pick one up to hold it, swap it for another, and swing it!
- **Chop trees and break rocks.** Felled trees and broken rocks grow back after a while.
- **Gather goodies:**
  - Logs.
  - Apples, peaches and coconuts.
  - Berries.
  - Herbs and mushrooms.
  - Potatoes, dug up with the shovel.

  Everything goes into one bag the whole group shares.
- **Light the campfire** with logs; each log keeps it burning for 3 minutes. Then **cook tasty dishes:**
  - Baked Potato.
  - Grilled Mushrooms.
  - Fruit Skewer.
  - Coconut Soup.
  - Berry Jam.
- **Take on quests** from the campfire. Gather everything on the list, and the whole group sits down for a **feast** with a little celebration.
- **New in this version:**
  - Four cozy background songs that play in turn. Press N or tap the music button to turn them on or off, and press M to mute everything.
  - A softer, gentler campfire crackle.
  - The version number now shows on the title screen.
