# What's new on Infinite Island 🌴

Your version number is shown under the title when the game starts.

---

## 0.31.6: The critters move in

- You can now **feed the hungry critters**! Carry a critter's favourite food (fox: snow truffle, squirrel: pinecone, rabbit: mint, grouse: lingonberry) and the prompt changes from "Hungry..." to **Feed**. It takes one food from the bag.
- The critter **eats, does a happy spin, scampers off and poofs**, then pops up at its own spot in the **Frostpine camp**. Everyone gets a toast ("A fox moved into the Frostpine camp! (3 / 8)"). Feed all eight!
- At the camp the critters potter about near the fire and turn to watch you. **At night they curl up and sleep close to the fire.** Press interact on one to **Pet** it and get a heart.
- Feeding works on every screen in a shared game (two players racing for one critter with one food: only one gets it), and the residents are saved: they are still at the camp after a reload, and new players joining see them there straight away.

## 0.31.5: Hungry critters

- Eight **hungry animals** live in the wild on Frostpine: two foxes, two squirrels, two rabbits and two grouse, scattered over the island (forest edges, the lake shore, the plateau, the far meadows and below the peaks). Go exploring to find them!
- Each one wanders a few steps around its home and blinks, hops and looks about. They are solid, so you can't walk through them.
- Walk up to one and it **hops and thinks of its favourite food** in a thought bubble: the fox wants a snow truffle, the squirrel a pinecone, the rabbit mint and the grouse a lingonberry. Their prompt says "Hungry..." for now; feeding them comes next.

## 0.31.4: The Emberleaf terrace garden

- Under the fire mountain on Emberleaf there is a **terrace garden**: three tiers of bare, dark volcanic soil with cracked old stone edging, a few weeds and a broken bamboo trellis. Nothing is planted yet, but it looks like somebody once kept a garden here.
- A **garden hearth** sits on the middle terrace: a ring of dark basalt stones with four flat stone seats. It is a real fire of its own: feed it logs, ask it for quests and **cook Emberleaf's four dishes** there (Grilled Banana, Taro & Coconut Bake, Pineapple Skewer, Breadfruit Roast). Once the Emberleaf bridge is built, **Emberleaf Luau** and **Jungle Skewer Picnic** can be your feast quest. **Prepare the feast** at the hearth and everyone sits on the stones with the fire mountain behind them.
- The **Hearth Spirit** says hello the first time you open the hearth's menu.
- A winding **earth trail** leads from the bridge landing to the garden (a good half-minute of running), with a **bamboo torch** every so often that glows at night. Signposts at the landing and the garden point the way ("Garden" one way, "Bridge" the other).
- Emberleaf now has **five tools** lying around: an axe and a shovel not far from the landing, a hammer and a sword along the trail and a spare shovel near the garden.
- Space is kept clear for what comes next: the garden's plots, and a waterfall and stream on the mountain's south-west side. Emberleaf's trees, rocks, flowers, pineapples and dig spots were nudged a little to make room.

## 0.31.3: The Frostpine camp

- Frostpine has a **camp**: a ring of snow-dusted stones round a camp fire, four tree-stump seats and a clearing of trampled snow, on the meadow a short run north of the bridge landing.
- A winding **snow trail** leads from the landing to the camp, with a wooden lantern post every so often that glows at night. Signposts at the landing and the camp point the way ("Camp" one way, "Bridge" the other).
- The camp fire is a real fire of its own: feed it logs, ask it for quests and **cook Frostpine's dishes** there (Forest Stew, Lingonberry Tart, Toasted Pine Nuts). Once the Frostpine bridge is built, **Frostpine Supper** and **Snowberry Tea Party** can be your feast quest.
- **Prepare the feast** at the camp and everyone sits on the stumps round the fire. The Frostpine Fire Spirit introduces itself the first time you open the camp fire's menu, and a **Camp jobs** row hints at what is coming.
- Frostpine's trees, boulders, flowers, berries, mushrooms and tools were nudged a little to make room for the camp and the trail.

## 0.31.1: Island kitchens

- Each island's campfire cooks only that island's own recipes. The home fire offers the five home dishes; Frostpine and Emberleaf dishes return when those islands get their own campfires.
- The four island feast quests wait for a campfire that can cook their dishes.
- The inventory bar no longer shows a scroll bar: it grows upward to fit, and the Bag lists everything.

## 0.31.0: Archipelago life

- Island-aware inventory chips, a complete shared Bag and Journal, and arrival toasts with bridge hysteresis.
- Four bridge-gated feast quests with their food models; screen-local Join a friend travel, far-end Homeward posts and named tether hints.
- Eighteen saved, synced Journal discoveries: nine beach glints plus rare shake / dig / rock finds. Album completion keeps hats tied to feasts.
- Frostpine's 3/4 Snowbell Waltz and Emberleaf Grove with log drums; music changes between tracks. Wind / owl and tropical bird / frog ambience.

## 0.30.0: Emberleaf Isle

- Explore a volcanic jungle with fruit palms, breadfruit trees, bamboo, ferns, vines, flowers, carved posts and a beach canoe.
- Gather bananas, breadfruit, bamboo, pineapples, taro and rare obsidian, and cook four new campfire dishes.
- Animated glowing lava, drifting smoke, a steaming hot spring with a small cascade and cosy sparkles, warm atmosphere and night fireflies.
- Gathering, harvesting and regrowth work across screens and survive visual rebuilds. The multiplayer stress check now accounts for Step 32's extra tools.

## 0.26.1

- **Keyboard players can leave.** In local multiplayer, the keyboard player's chip in the top-left now has a small leave button. Click it to step out (anything you were holding drops to the ground) while everyone else keeps playing. Press any key to jump back in.

## 0.26.0: Feast hats

- **A new hat for every feast.** Finish a quest feast and you unlock a hat, up to ten: a frying pan, a mushroom cap, a stock pot, a coconut, a chef's toque, an apple, a butterfly, a teakettle, a lily pad and a golden feast crown.
- **The hat rack.** After your first feast a hat rack pops up behind the campfire benches. Your unlocked hats hang on it. Walk up and press Interact to try the next hat on, then the plain beanie again after the last one.
- Everyone can do it, even with a tool in hand, and everybody on every screen sees the hats you wear. Your choice is remembered on your device.

## 0.25.2

- **Chopping can shake fruit loose.** Every swing at a fruit tree has a small chance to knock an apple, peach or coconut down, and felled fruit trees often drop a bit of fruit too.
- **Add several logs in a row.** Choosing "Add a log" at the campfire no longer closes the menu, so you can keep tossing logs in. The option greys out once the fire is roaring.

## 0.25.1

- **Fixed:** characters sometimes showed up as flat, single-colour shapes with no face. Everyone looks like themselves again.
- **Fixed:** if a friend's phone reconnected while they were holding a tool, the tool could vanish. It now drops to the ground where you can pick it up again.

## 0.25.0: Play together from your own phone

- **Play on your own phone.** Scan the QR code from the 📱 button and choose:
  - **Use as controller:** your phone becomes a gamepad for the shared screen, like before.
  - **Play on this phone:** you get your own camera and can explore anywhere on the island, while still playing with everyone else.
- **See each other.** Players on other devices appear as their own characters, walking around and holding their tools.
- **Up to 4 islanders**, however you mix them: keyboard, controllers, phone controllers or phones with their own view.
- **Feast together.** When a quest feast starts, everyone gathers around the fire at the same moment, wherever they are.
- **Everyone shares one island.** Trees you chop, items you pick up and the time of day are the same for everyone.
- **Paused when the host steps away.** If the device that started the game is put away or switches apps, the island pauses for everyone until it comes back.
- **See yourself behind trees.** When a tree or rock hides your character, a coloured outline shows where you are.
- **Better on phones:**
  - Layouts are tidier in both upright and sideways views.
  - The camera frames your character so your thumbs don't cover them.
  - The game runs more smoothly.
- **Night is more magical:**
  - Twinkling stars.
  - Softly glowing mushrooms.
  - Moonlit sparkles on the sea.
  - Birdsong by day and crickets at night.
  - Music that suits the time of day.

## 0.21.0

- **Behind the scenes:** characters can now wear swappable hats. Hats themselves are coming in a future update!

## 0.19.2

- **Brighter nights.** Night is a little lighter, so it still feels like night but you can see where you're going.

## 0.19.1

- **Easier campfire menus with the phone sideways.** The menu now uses the full height of the screen, so all the options fit.

## 0.19.0: Day & night

- **A full day on the island** every 10½ minutes:
  - Pink and peach dawns.
  - Sunny days.
  - Orange sunsets.
  - Cozy, moonlit nights.
- The sun and moon cross the sky, shadows stretch at sunset, and the water and clouds change colour through the day.
- **The campfire glows brighter at night**, a perfect spot to gather.
- **A little clock** in the corner shows the time, with a friendly message as each part of the day begins.
- The time of day is saved, so the island picks up where you left it.
- **Better on touch screens:**
  - All the buttons can be tapped properly.
  - Menus sit above the on-screen controls.
  - Options are tucked into a tidy ⋯ menu.
  - In solo play, a **+** button lets friends join.

## 0.16.0: Welcome to Infinite Island!

The first numbered version of the island. Here's what you can do:

- **Explore a cozy island:**
  - A sandy beach, meadows and a little plateau.
  - A pond and a stream.
  - Cherry blossom trees, palms, flowers, butterflies and drifting petals.
- **Play your way:**
  - Keyboard (WASD to move, E to interact, Q to cancel).
  - A gamepad, including GameCube and Wii controller adapters.
  - Your phone as a wireless controller: just scan the QR code.
- **Up to 4 friends on one screen**, each with their own colour.
- **Find tools around the island:** an axe, sword, hammer, shovel and three lightsabers. Pick one up to hold it, swap it for another, and swing it!
- **Chop trees and break rocks.** Felled trees and broken rocks grow back after a while.
- **Gather goodies:**
  - Logs.
  - Apples, peaches and coconuts.
  - Berries.
  - Herbs and mushrooms.
  - Potatoes, dug up with the shovel.

  Everything goes into one bag the whole group shares.
- **Light the campfire** with logs; each log keeps it burning for 3 minutes. Then **cook tasty dishes:**
  - Baked Potato.
  - Grilled Mushrooms.
  - Fruit Skewer.
  - Coconut Soup.
  - Berry Jam.
- **Take on quests** from the campfire. Gather everything on the list, and the whole group sits down for a **feast** with a little celebration.
- **New in this version:**
  - Four cozy background songs that play in turn. Press N or tap the music button to turn them on or off, and press M to mute everything.
  - A softer, gentler campfire crackle.
  - The version number now shows on the title screen.
